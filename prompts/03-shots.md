# 03 · The two shots, as approved

Eight drafts on Seedance 2.5 at 480p led to these two. Both were then finished in 1080p from the draft itself, so the finish is the same shot at full quality, not a new generation. Each prompt binds the character sheet as @Image1 and, for the first, the grey room as @Image2.

## Shot 1 · the before (6 seconds, 35mm close-up)

```
A live-action cinematic close-up in a home office on a grey rainy afternoon: a freelancer deletes a message, breathes out, and puts his phone down.

[Asset bindings]
@Image1 is Tom: short dark curly hair, short dark beard. Refer to the head panel for his face only.
@Image2 is the location: his home office on an overcast rainy day. Refer to it for the room and light only.

[Scene setting]
Tom is alone, sitting squarely at his oak desk and facing it, in a faded grey T-shirt. A 35mm lens close to him: his face large in the frame, the room falling away behind him in soft focus, straight lines staying straight. Muted green and amber grade, cold window light on one side of his face against the warm brass desk lamp on the other, fine film grain.

[Shot list]
Shot 1: One continuous shot; the camera does not cut on its own. The camera is low, at desk height, at a three-quarter angle, looking up at Tom's face past the phone he holds in both hands above the desk; the phone's top edge is soft at the bottom of the frame. The camera is still, with a faint handheld breath. Tom looks down at the phone, his thumb pressed on the screen, deleting. He stops. He closes his eyes for a moment and breathes out slowly. He opens his eyes, lowers the phone out of the bottom of the frame and sets it on the desk, and stays looking down at it. Sound: rain on the glass, soft keyboard ticks, one quiet breath, the phone touching wood.

[Overall requirements]
Played tiny and real: his eyes and his breath, nothing more; his expression stays flat, no smile. Tom never looks into the lens. Tom's face remains stable without deformation. No BGM, no voiceover — only environmental and action sounds. Avoid generating any text or subtitles; do not generate a logo or a watermark.
```

Only 1.6 seconds of it are in the ad: the pain is glimpsed, not held.

## Shot 2 · the after (10 seconds, one slow pull-back)

```
A live-action cinematic oner at golden hour: the camera starts outside a freelancer's open window, on his face, and pulls back and away from the building while he works with a quiet smile.

[Asset bindings]
@Image1 is Tom: short dark curly hair, short dark beard, a terracotta overshirt over a white T-shirt. Refer to it for his appearance.

[Scene setting]
An upper-floor home office in a beautiful new white building with clean balconies. The oak desk is pushed up against the window sill under the large open window. Tom sits at the desk facing the window, so he faces the camera. His monitor stands on the desk at the sill with its back to the window, low enough that his face is above it; from the window only the monitor's plain back and Tom's face are seen, nothing else between the camera and him. His phone lies screen-up on the desk beside the monitor, on the window side. Behind Tom is the rest of the room: a bookshelf, a monstera, an orange poster on the wall, a terrazzo floor, a brass desk lamp beside him. Low warm sun across his face. 35mm lens, warm amber and green film colour, fine film grain.

[Shot list]
Shot 1: One continuous shot; the camera does not cut on its own. The opening frame is a close-up of Tom's face alone, filling the frame, from a three-quarter angle to his right; he works, focused, with a small content smile. The camera pulls back slowly along that same diagonal and keeps the angle for the whole shot: the back of the monitor enters from the left edge of the frame and settles in the lower left, the desk and the phone come into view, the window frame and sill enter around the edges, the camera crosses the window plane into the open air, and the white facade grows around his window seen slightly from the right, never head-on. Tom stays at the centre of the frame to the end, small, working in his window with the room behind him. As the desk comes into view, the phone buzzes once and its screen lights up with a small notification; Tom's eyes drop to it for a moment and return to his screen, and he keeps typing, his smile unchanged. Sound: soft typing, one phone buzz on wood, then birds, a breeze, the distant city.

[Overall requirements]
Calm and content, a quiet smile throughout, never a grin; Tom never looks into the lens; the glance at the phone is small and brief. One camera move at one constant slow speed, no turn. Tom's face remains stable without deformation. No BGM, no voiceover — only environmental and action sounds. No legible text on the phone. Avoid generating any text or subtitles; do not generate a logo or a watermark.
```

Five seconds of it carry the closing line. Notice what the prompt does that the failed drafts did not: it states where the desk, the monitor and the window are relative to each other and to the camera, in plain geometry, before it describes any acting.

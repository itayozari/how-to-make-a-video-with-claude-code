# 01 · The first prompt

Tell Claude what the video is for and where it can learn about the product. It can be this simple:

```
Hi, I want to make an ad for Jerv. Here's a link to a doc that explains the app. Help me figure out which video is right to make, write the script with me, and then produce it.
```

With Tonpit connected, Claude asks the right questions, helps you decide what kind of video is worth making, sharpens the message and writes the script with you. Nothing is generated until you approve the script.

## Or start from a template

Tonpit has ready video structures as starting points: you and Claude fit your product into one and change whatever needs changing. The Jerv ad is the Before / After ad template. For the shape of a brief that fits it, see [BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md](../BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md).

## What the first hour looked like

The Jerv session started from a rough table of shots. Before a single credit was spent, the first hour went into the script and the plan:

- Claude pushed back on four points of the table; two were accepted, two became a different idea.
- Two planned shots turned out to be one shot, under eight seconds.
- The production split was decided: a character sheet on Seedream 5, the location on GPT Image 2.5, all footage on Seedance 2.5 starting at 480p.
- The images came first, approved before any video was generated.

Invest in the script. Everything after it is a result of its quality.
